# USA Regional Sales Performance Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a fictional company’s U.S. regional sales data from 2014–2018 using Python, SQL, Jupyter Notebook, and Power BI.

The objective was to uncover revenue drivers, profitability trends, customer behavior, seasonal patterns, and regional performance insights to support business decision-making.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement

Analyze sales data to identify:

* Top-performing products, channels, and regions
* Seasonal sales trends and anomalies
* Profitability drivers and pricing risks
* Customer concentration and revenue distribution
* Opportunities for growth and market expansion

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Perform data cleaning and preprocessing
* Conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA)
* Generate business insights from sales data
* Analyze revenue, profit, pricing, and customer trends
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for decision-making

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The project includes:

* Monthly Sales Trend Analysis
* Seasonal Trend Detection
* Top Products by Revenue
* Profit Margin Analysis
* Channel Performance Analysis
* Customer Segmentation
* Correlation Heatmap
* State-wise Revenue Analysis
* Geospatial Sales Visualization

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* California generated the highest revenue and order volume.
* Wholesale contributed more than 50% of total sales.
* Export channel delivered the highest average profit margins.
* Revenue showed stable seasonal patterns with a notable dip in early 2017.
* Profitability was strongly influenced by unit pricing.
* Revenue concentration among top customers highlighted retention risks.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* Expand export-focused marketing and partnerships.
* Investigate revenue anomalies from early 2017.
* Optimize pricing strategies for lower-performing products.
* Increase regional growth initiatives in underperforming states.
* Improve customer retention strategies for top revenue-generating accounts.
